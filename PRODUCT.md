# Product

## Register

product

## Current Role
UPL Lens is currently a maintained Uganda Premier League analytical data foundation and practical casework system. The former fan-facing public product is retained/frozen as useful code, design knowledge, API/frontend contract history, and portfolio evidence, but it is no longer the automatic destination for new UPL analysis.

## Users
The active users are the maintainer, analytical caseworkers, future contributors, and reviewers who need trustworthy UPL source acquisition, validated Postgres data, reproducible notebooks, clear caveats, and closed case outputs. Retained public-product users remain stats-curious UPL fans, analysts, journalists, and technical reviewers when the existing frontend/API assets are maintained or demonstrated.

## Product Purpose
UPL Lens turns official UPL match data into trustworthy, reproducible football intelligence. Success now means a bounded football question can be answered from maintained data contracts with a case-specific reproducibility record, checks, notebook evidence, findings/report, outputs, caveats, and a hard endpoint.

## Retained Product Phase
The completed public-product phase proved the full path from source pages through scraping, Postgres, FastAPI, React, hosted operations, QA, and public presentation. Its design and trust lessons remain valuable for retained frontend/API maintenance or explicitly approved future presentation work.

## Brand Personality
Neutral, credible, analytical. The voice should feel calm and evidence-led: clear enough for everyday fans when public surfaces are used, serious enough for analysts, and honest about data limits.

## Anti-references
- Treating every notebook finding as a required frontend/API deliverable.
- Generic admin-template dashboards with no football story.
- Marketing-first landing pages that hide the analytical workflow.
- Visual styles that prioritize decoration over readability and data trust.
- Interfaces that mimic official fixtures pages instead of adding insight.

## Design Principles
- Evidence first: every case or retained screen should surface football meaning before raw numbers.
- Trust by default: show caveats, freshness, coverage, and uncertainty visibly when relevant.
- Bounded work: start from a real question and end with a clear case endpoint unless software promotion is separately approved.
- Progressive depth: provide clear findings first, then reproducible notebooks, checks, and source records.
- Product lessons retained: demonstrate technical quality through reliability and clarity, not self-promotion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
For retained public surfaces, target WCAG AA as a baseline. Keep readable contrast, clear focus states, keyboard-accessible interactions, and reduced-motion support. Prioritize mobile readability and touch-friendly controls for broad device access.
